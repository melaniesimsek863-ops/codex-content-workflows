# Long Series Continuity

Long series fail when memory breaks. Maintain a ledger before writing many episodes.

## Required Ledgers

### Character Ledger

Track every major character's desire, secret, knowledge, relationship state, and visual continuity.

### Timeline Ledger

Track episode date/order, location, irreversible events, injuries, promises, and deadlines.

### Mystery Ledger

Track clues, false clues, answers, who knows each answer, and when the audience learns it.

### Hook And Payoff Ledger

Every hook creates debt. Record:

- hook
- episode introduced
- expected payoff window
- actual payoff
- consequence

### Power Ledger

For power, business, family, school, palace, survival, or organization stories, track who controls resources, information, enforcement, legitimacy, and exit routes.

## Arc Control

Each arc must have:

- central question
- main opponent
- protagonist's wrong strategy
- midpoint reversal
- cost escalation
- final choice
- permanent change

## Anti-Collapse Rules

- Do not introduce a new mystery to avoid paying off an old one.
- Do not solve a core problem by coincidence unless coincidence creates a worse problem.
- Do not let a side character know information they never learned.
- Do not change a rule after it becomes inconvenient; reveal a deeper rule instead.
- Do not let the protagonist forget a lesson without emotional reason.
