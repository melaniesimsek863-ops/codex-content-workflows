# Cangjie Method Map

Use the local Cangjie pack as optional method support. Read exact source skills only when the user's problem needs that layer.

Cangjie root: the user's configured `<CANGJIE_LIBRARY>` directory, if available.

## Story Depth

Source: `06_文学历史类(literature-history)\我与地坛(wo-yu-di-tan-skill)`

- `writing-zero-start`: start writing from scene, question, and unavoidable confusion.
- `suffering-without-chicken-soup`: write pain without cheap redemption.
- `belated-empathy-forward`: write regret, family, love, delayed understanding, and repair.
- `limit-bound-action`: make characters act within real constraints.

## Character And Power

Source: `06_文学历史类(literature-history)\明朝那些事儿(mingchao-those-things-skill)`

- `actor-position-analysis`: design character from position, resource, constraint, opponent, and goal.
- `power-structure-diagnosis`: build organizations, families, schools, buildings, companies, clans, and hidden controllers.
- `information-distortion-defense`: create misinformation, rumors, hidden channels, false reports, and reveal mechanics.
- `popular-history-translation`: turn complex background into character line and causal line.

## Virality

Source: `03_文案营销类(copywriting-marketing)\疯传(contagious-skill)`

- `stepps-diagnostic`: diagnose share potential.
- `social-currency`: make viewers feel smart, stylish, kind, brave, or emotionally perceptive when sharing.
- `emotion-arousal-model`: use high-arousal emotion: awe, anger, anxiety, surprise, amusement, desire.
- `trojan-horse`: embed theme, value, or account identity inside the story.
- `trigger-habitat`: connect story symbols to everyday reminders.

## Psychology

Source: `03_文案营销类(copywriting-marketing)\影响力(influence-skill)`

- `commitment-consistency`: traps created by prior promises.
- `social-proof`: crowd pressure, online judgment, collective belief.
- `authority`: authority pressure and false authority.
- `scarcity`: limited opportunity, one-time door, disappearing person, final train.
- `contrast-principle`: emotional and status contrast.

## AI Workflow

Source: `05_技术工具类(tech-tools)\165个系统提示词(system-prompt-skills)`
