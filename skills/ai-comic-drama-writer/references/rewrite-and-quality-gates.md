# Rewrite And Quality Gates

Diagnose before rewriting. Identify the failing layer, then revise that layer.

## Diagnosis Layers

1. Premise: Is the core question strong?
2. Character: Does the protagonist want something specific and costly?
3. Conflict: Is there active opposition?
4. Stakes: What gets worse if they fail?
5. Structure: Does each beat cause the next?
6. Emotion: Is the feeling earned rather than declared?
7. Visuality: Can the scene be drawn or filmed?
8. Dialogue: Do lines carry action and subtext?
9. Originality: Is it structurally fresh enough?
10. Retention: Does the audience have a reason to continue?
11. Human voice: Does the dialogue or narration still sound like AI-generated text?

## Quality Gates

A draft is not ready if any answer is no:

- Can the premise be explained in one sharp sentence?
- Does the first scene create immediate curiosity or pressure?
- Does the protagonist make a risky choice?
- Does the antagonist or opposing force have credible advantage?
- Does each scene change information, power, emotion, or danger?
- Is there at least one memorable visual anchor?
- Does the ending create payoff or a strong next question?
- For AI comic: can every panel be drawn without guessing basic visuals?
- For long series: are hooks recorded and scheduled for payoff?
- For dialogue and narration: does each line sound like a specific person under pressure rather than a neutral assistant?

## Rewrite Passes

Use focused passes:

- Hook pass: rewrite first 3 seconds or first page.
- Conflict pass: add opposition and cost.
- Character pass: deepen wound, desire, secret, and active choice.
- Visual pass: replace abstract narration with drawable action.
- Dialogue pass: shorten lines and add subtext.
- Continuity pass: check ledgers.
- Viral pass: strengthen title, emotion, share reason, and comment bait.
- Human voice pass: remove AI-shaped filler, generic emotional labels, formulaic rhythm, and assistant residue while preserving plot facts.

## Scoring Rubric

Score 1-5:

- hook strength
- protagonist agency
- conflict pressure
- emotional truth
- visual clarity
- originality
- genre satisfaction
- episode retention
- production readiness
- human voice

Below 3 requires rewriting before final delivery.

## Human Voice Gate

Use `human-voice-pass.md` after structure, character, and continuity are already acceptable. This gate edits language only. It should not change plot logic, clues, character knowledge, storyboard fields, or image prompts.

Apply it especially to:

- dialogue that explains too much
- narration that summarizes emotion instead of showing behavior
- hooks that sound like generic marketing copy
- captions with assistant-like phrasing
- endings with vague positive closure
