# Short Video Drama

Short video drama is retention-first storytelling. The first seconds must create a question, danger, shock, desire, or contradiction.

## Duration Patterns

### 15 Seconds

- 0-2s: hook image or impossible line
- 2-8s: conflict and context
- 8-13s: reversal
- 13-15s: punchline or cliffhanger

### 30 Seconds

- 0-3s: hook
- 3-10s: who wants what
- 10-20s: obstacle escalates
- 20-27s: twist
- 27-30s: next-episode hook or emotional sting

### 60 Seconds

- 0-3s: hook
- 3-12s: setup through conflict
- 12-30s: pursuit or confrontation
- 30-45s: reveal
- 45-55s: consequence
- 55-60s: hook

## Hook Types

- forbidden message
- impossible visual
- public humiliation
- secret identity leak
- countdown
- reversed relationship
- rule announcement
- strange neighbor
- betrayal line
- future warning

## Retention Rules

- Put the question before the explanation.
- Delay backstory until the viewer wants it.
- Make every cut answer one question and raise another.
- Keep names few in early seconds.
- Use concrete objects as plot anchors: phone, key, door, elevator card, photo, contract, medicine, invoice.

## Short Script Output

For short video scripts, output:

1. title options
2. logline
3. duration plan
4. script with timestamp ranges
5. shot list
6. captions/subtitles
7. cover text
8. comment bait question
