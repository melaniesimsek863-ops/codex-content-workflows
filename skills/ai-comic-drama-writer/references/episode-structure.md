# Episode Structure

## One-Shot Structure

Use for short stories or single episodes:

1. Hook image or line
2. Normal world under pressure
3. Inciting disruption
4. First choice
5. Reversal
6. Cost
7. Final choice
8. Emotional or ironic aftertaste

## Short Series Structure

For 3-10 episodes:

- Episode 1: impossible hook, protagonist wound, immediate crisis
- Episode 2: first rule or opponent logic, small win with cost
- Episode 3: relationship complication, new danger
- Middle: one reveal per episode, one reversal per episode
- Penultimate: protagonist's old method fails
- Finale: protagonist wins by changing method or paying price

## Long Series Structure

For 10+ episodes, organize arcs:

- Arc A: entry and survival
- Arc B: power or mystery expands
- Arc C: protagonist becomes active player
- Arc D: truth costs more than ignorance
- Arc E: final confrontation and transformed identity

## Episode Card

Each episode needs:

- episode number
- title
- purpose in series
- opening hook
- protagonist goal
- obstacle
- new information
- reversal
- emotional beat
- ending hook
- continuity changes

## Ending Hook Types

- revelation: a hidden fact is exposed
- threat: danger arrives now
- choice: protagonist must choose under time pressure
- betrayal: trusted person changes side
- visual shock: impossible image
- emotional cliff: confession, breakup, recognition, death-like loss
- rule break: known rule fails or reverses
