# Genre Playbooks

Use genre as an audience contract. Fulfill the contract, then add one fresh angle.

## Suspense / Strange Building / Rule Mystery

Contract: danger, clues, rules, paranoia, delayed reveal.

Required beats:

1. ordinary entry into abnormal space
2. first rule or anomaly
3. small violation with visible consequence
4. false explanation
5. deeper pattern
6. personal connection to mystery
7. rule inversion or hidden controller

Useful devices: restricted floor, repeating room, unreliable notice, neighbor secrets, forbidden elevator, management office, old ledger, surveillance blind spot.

## Revenge / Comeback

Contract: humiliation, hidden strength, preparation, reversal, catharsis.

Required beats:

1. unfair injury
2. protagonist loses visible status
3. secret resource or painful competence
4. opponent overconfidence
5. trap or test
6. public reversal
7. cost of revenge

Avoid: effortless victory, foolish opponent, no moral price.

## Romance / Sweet-Abuse / Relationship

Contract: longing, misunderstanding, intimacy, hurt, choice.

Required beats:

1. attraction or old wound
2. external barrier
3. private tenderness
4. misread signal
5. irreversible hurt or sacrifice
6. truth reveal
7. choice under cost

Avoid: conflict that could be solved by one normal sentence unless the reason for silence is strong.

## Urban Fantasy / Supernatural

Contract: hidden rule, special ability, social consequence, power price.

Required beats:

1. impossible event
2. rule discovery
3. first use
4. side effect
5. rival or watcher
6. rule loophole
7. larger system revealed

## Family / Ethics / Realistic Drama

Contract: recognition, conflict of duty and self, painful truth, grounded emotion.

Required beats:

1. everyday trigger
2. family role pressure
3. old wound returns
4. practical dilemma
5. private confession or collapse
6. boundary-setting action
7. bittersweet change

## Comedy / Satire

Contract: recognizable pain, exaggeration, rhythm, punchline, reversal.

Required beats:

1. familiar situation
2. distorted rule
3. escalation through logic
4. status reversal
5. punchline with truth

## Sci-fi / AI / Future Society

Contract: concept, consequence, human choice.

Required beats:

1. speculative rule
2. personal use case
3. unintended consequence
4. social system reaction
5. ethical tradeoff
6. irreversible decision
