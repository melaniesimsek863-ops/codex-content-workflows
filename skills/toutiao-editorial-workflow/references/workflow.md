# Workflow

## Article Flow

1. Define positioning, audience, content pillars, boundaries, and commercial path.
2. Capture a reader problem and a distinct angle in a topic card.
3. Build an evidence pack from current, traceable sources; prefer official primary sources for time-sensitive claims.
4. When a style reference is requested, build a multi-source style profile and separate transferable mechanisms from protected source expression.
5. Write the thesis and outline before drafting.
6. Generate three `开头候选` using genuinely different mechanisms, select one against the reader problem, then draft only from the approved brief and evidence pack.
7. Run `读者评审`: identify likely confusion, distrust, unanswered questions, and reasons to stop reading. Revise the argument before surface polishing.
8. Generate title variants, then test accuracy, completeness, reader benefit, and platform compliance.
9. Mark facts, quotations, names, numbers, links, and other protected spans. Apply the final human-voice pass without changing their meaning.
10. Re-run fact checks on the exact final body and bind the passing result to that file's `SHA-256`.
11. Run platform, rights, originality, AI-label, and final human-approval gates.
12. Publish manually, record 24-hour and 72-hour metrics snapshots, then write one-variable experiment results back to the method library.

## Human And AI Roles

AI may cluster topics, summarize sources, propose angles, draft outlines, generate title variants, check structure, and suggest experiments. The human owns source selection, factual interpretation, original contribution, personal experience, rights, final voice, and the publish action.

## Style Research Rules

- Successful writers and accounts are valid research samples. Similarity in topic, genre conventions, common terminology, and proven reader-facing structures is not treated as a defect by itself.
- Prefer several authors or accounts plus several works per source. Record what performed well and whether the reason is known or only inferred.
- Convert source patterns into an account-owned profile. Do not paste a named style preset directly into a publication prompt.
- Preserve the new article's own thesis, evidence selection, reasoning, examples, and wording.

## Evidence Rules

- Record publisher, URL, published date, access date, claim, and verification state.
- Distinguish direct evidence, reported claims, course opinions, and agent inference.
- For medical, legal, financial, political, safety, or current policy claims, use current authoritative sources and stronger review.
- Reject unsupported precision, unnamed experts, fabricated quotes, and circular citations.

## Experiment Rules

Keep raw metrics immutable. Compare like-for-like content windows. Change one primary variable at a time, such as topic angle, title, opening, or visual. Treat small samples as directional rather than conclusive.
