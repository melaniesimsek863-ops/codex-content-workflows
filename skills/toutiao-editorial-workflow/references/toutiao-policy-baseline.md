# Toutiao Policy Baseline

Last verified: 2026-08-12. Refresh before a real publication.

## Current Baseline

- Toutiao's AI creation notice prohibits harmful, unverified, false, infringing, and otherwise non-compliant AI-assisted content.
- The article originality help page updated 2026-02-25 lists AI-created content among content that cannot claim the article originality label.
- The content creation rules restrict repeat uploads, low-effort semantic rewrites, stitched compilations, exaggerated titles, title-body mismatch, and malformed title punctuation.
